What is the exit load for SBI Bluechip?
smart_toy
Assistant

For investments in the SBI Large Cap Fund (formerly Bluechip), an exit load of 1% is payable if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment. No exit load is levied for redemptions after 1 year.
